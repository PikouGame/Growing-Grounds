Growing Grounds 0.1.5-alpha

- Base Upgrade previews bonus chest slots, with a compact chest icon.
- Set the range or chest cap to 0 (Off) to disable that bonus and hide its row.
- Next-radius metres turn turquoise when the upgrade is ready; current metres stay mint.
- Reward spacing expands upward to fit all four rows.
- World Settings retains the live menu correctly and preserves Off/bonus labels after slider changes.

Update the host/server and clients together. Occupied extra chest slots remain accessible until emptied when lowering the cap. No developer replay shortcuts are included.

We'd love to hear how it works in your world. Share your experience or report bugs so we can investigate and improve Growing Grounds together:
https://github.com/PikouGame/Growing-Grounds/issues
