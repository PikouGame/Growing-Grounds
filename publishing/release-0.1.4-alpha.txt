Growing Grounds 0.1.4-alpha

More room for your supplies: chests gain one slot per base upgrade from level 2,
up to 20 bonus slots by default. Guild Chests receive the bonus once for their
shared inventory. Set the bonus cap from 0 to 40 in World Settings or config.lua.
Lowering the cap keeps occupied extra slots accessible until you empty them.

The range cap now clearly uses bonuses: +50% to +300%, with +100% as the default.
Your old settings keep the same effective radius. +100% doubles the radius;
+300% makes it four times larger. The default growth remains 5% per level.

Update both host/server and clients. Back up your world, preserve your existing
config.lua and world-settings.ini, and add the new options from the supplied
config as needed. There are no development keyboard shortcuts.

Checked with native inventories: growth, save/reload, garbage collection,
client/server transfers and occupied-slot protection across a server restart.
The three World Settings rows were checked in the normal new-world menu.
Full world-creation/save flows and native Windows OS execution remain unverified.

We'd love your comments, experiences and bug reports so we can investigate and
improve Growing Grounds: https://github.com/PikouGame/Growing-Grounds/issues
