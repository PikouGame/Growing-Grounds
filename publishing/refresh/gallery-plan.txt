Publishing the description images
The descriptions load their images from GitHub at a pinned ref. New cards are pushed first;
then regenerate with
  python3 tools/showcase_listings.py --asset-base https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PikouGame/Growing-Grounds/<commit>/assets/showcase/refresh --sync-readme
and copy publishing/refresh/description.* over publishing/description.* in a second commit.
This refresh pins the images to d399f7e. Nexus takes description.bbcode, CurseForge
description.md.
