Growing Grounds 0.2.1-beta — choose your progression

- Choose your starting Palbox boxes and TOTAL cap, from 1 to 96. Defaults are 32 at base level 1 and 64 at level 35, spread across the levels. Occupied, locked and open boxes are retained when reducing capacity. Global Palbox is unchanged.
- Restore independent range gain per upgrade (default 4%, 0–20%) and maximum bonus (default 100%, 0–300%). Five GG controls sit beside the native base/building settings, with the miniature GG mark.
- Keep chest bonuses spread across base levels: 18 by default, maximum 42 in steps of six. Guild Chests share one bonus.
- Fix reward-row reconstruction and late/recreated World Settings widgets. Centralize setting definitions without changing migrations, saved format or intended values. Retain packaging validation and integrated reward-flow tests.
- Archive GG automatic backups and remove their companion, bundled Python, launch bootstrap and backup-only tools. Existing archives and Palworld's own backups are preserved.
- Refresh the presentation with the native level11 upgrade menu and the approved range/Palbox illustrations.

Updating
Close the game/server and keep a world backup. If 0.2.0-beta used the GG Proton/server launcher, remove that wrapper while preserving other launch options, and let any pending old backup finish. Preserve config.lua and world-settings.ini / .bak, then REPLACE the old BaseRangeGrowth folder instead of merging it. Do not copy back the old tools/, backup/ or backup-version.txt. Keep existing archive directories. Update host/server and clients together; see INSTALL.txt.

The restored range setting can change existing radii. Review both gain and cap. Legacy Palbox bonus caps migrate to a total of 32 + the old bonus; an explicit total cap takes precedence. Starting with fewer boxes is optional.

Validation
Default range, chest and total-Palbox rewards, their dedicated-server preview and the five-row menu were checked natively through Proton/Wine. Automated regression checks cover the integrated settings/calculation/replication/UI flow, default equivalence, migrations, storage retention and packaging. The latest audit refactor has not had a native game session; smaller starting Palbox capacities/save-reload, physical Windows, Guild Chest UI and Workshop installation remain unverified. No FPS benchmark is claimed.
