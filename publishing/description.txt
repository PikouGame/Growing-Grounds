Growing Grounds
Room to grow. A moment to celebrate.

Give your base room to grow: +5% of its original radius per level from level 2, up to 200% by default. See your current and next range bonus, with metres beside each value, in the Base Upgrade menu, then watch a blue wave travel from the Palbox to the expanding boundary.

Animation: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PikouGame/Growing-Grounds/e38ca7230c31f1d73453178c17e04f3f64f3b0fb/assets/showcase/growing-grounds-upgrade-web.gif
Gameplay excerpt at original speed; initial wait trimmed.

Make it your own
Choose 0–20% growth per level and a 100–200% cap. Local hosts get sliders in World Settings; dedicated-server owners use config.lua. Nearby bases can limit expansion, and new Palboxes reserve room for future growth.

Getting started
Requires a compatible Palworld UE4SS loader on the host/server and clients.

1. Close the game/server and back up your world.
2. Copy BaseRangeGrowth into your existing UE4SS Mods folder on both sides.
3. Keep your config.lua and world-settings.ini when updating.

Updating from an older version? The 200% cap and spacing limits may reduce an existing base’s range. See INSTALL.txt for full instructions.

A small note
This is an alpha. The celebration can still start late after closing the Palbox. Tested on Palworld v1.0.5.102999 using Windows builds through Proton/Wine; see the README for compatibility notes.
