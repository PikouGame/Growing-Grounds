Growing Grounds
Room to grow. A moment to celebrate.

Give your base room to grow: +5% of its original radius per level from level 2, up to a +100% bonus by default (double the radius). See your current and next range bonus, with metres beside each value, in the Base Upgrade menu, then watch a blue wave travel from the Palbox to the expanding boundary.

More room for your supplies, too: chests gain one slot per base upgrade, up to 20 bonus slots by default. Guild Chests receive the bonus once for their shared inventory.

Make it your own
Choose 0–20% growth per level and a bonus cap from 0 to +300%. +100% doubles the radius; +300% makes it four times larger. Local hosts get sliders in World Settings; dedicated-server owners use config.lua. Nearby bases can limit expansion, and new Palboxes reserve room for future growth.

The bonus chest cap is adjustable from 0 to 40. Set either cap to 0 (Off) to disable that bonus. Lowering it keeps occupied extra slots accessible until you empty them.

Getting started
Requires a compatible Palworld UE4SS loader on the host/server and clients. Use a build with game-thread timers, such as the tested Palworld fork 2281fa31; exact API requirements are in INSTALL.txt.

1. Close the game/server and back up your world.
2. Copy BaseRangeGrowth into your existing UE4SS Mods folder on both sides.
3. Keep your config.lua and world-settings.ini when updating.

Existing configurations keep their previous range cap. See INSTALL.txt for full instructions.

A small note
This is an alpha. The celebration can still start late after closing the Palbox. Tested on Palworld v1.0.5.102999 using Windows builds through Proton/Wine; see the README for compatibility notes.
