Growing Grounds
Room to grow. A moment to celebrate.

Give your base a little more space each time it levels up. Growing Grounds adds
5% of the original radius per level, starting at level 2, with a default cap of
200%. The Base Upgrade screen shows your current and next range in a native
reward row with an original four-arrow icon.

Watch your base grow
A blue wave travels from the Palbox across the terrain. It meets the boundary,
which expands with a gentle bounce, then a glowing contour rises and fades with
its trail. If Base Upgrade is open, the effect prepares after the upgrade and
waits for you to close the screen, so you can see the celebration.

Make it fit your world
Growth per level is adjustable from 0–20%; the cap from 100–200%. Local worlds
have two Growing Grounds sliders at the bottom of custom World Settings.
Dedicated-server owners use config.lua. Nearby bases can limit growth further,
and new Palboxes reserve space for future expansion. A standard 35 m base reaches
49 m at level 9 and, with default settings, the 70 m cap at level 21.

Getting started
Requires a compatible UE4SS loader for Palworld, installed separately. Install
Growing Grounds on the host/server and clients. Close the game/server, keep a
world backup, and copy BaseRangeGrowth into your existing UE4SS Mods folder.
Update both sides and preserve config.lua preferences and world-settings.ini.
Full paths and reset instructions are included in INSTALL.txt.

Updating from 0.1.1? The new 200% ceiling and spacing limits can reduce a previously
expanded base. Buildings outside the new boundary may lose base coverage, so
please keep a backup before updating.

An evolving alpha
Known visual issue: the celebration can still start late after closing the Palbox. This does not postpone the actual base-radius upgrade. A new lightweight change observer is included; its improvement during a natural upgrade has not yet been verified in game.

Tested on Palworld v1.0.5.102999 with UE4SS fork 2281fa31, using Windows builds
through Proton/Wine. Radius replication, the 140% -> 145% reward preview, and
the screen-close animation replay were checked in game. This update also replaces
the repeated object searches behind the reported five-second freezes on the test setup.

We are still gathering feedback on World Settings save flows, new-base placement,
Pal work and resource access in expanded areas, the full reset lifecycle, other
range mods, and native Windows OS/hardware combinations. Workshop deployment
remains unverified. The added row is currently English. When the server's next
range is unavailable, the row shows only the current value.
