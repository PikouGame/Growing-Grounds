Growing Grounds 0.2.0-beta — more room for your Pals

- Your regular Palbox gains one extra box per base upgrade. Default cap: 32 extra boxes; configurable from 0 to 64. Level 1 gives none, level 33 reaches the default cap. At level 35, a higher cap can grant at most 34 extra boxes. Global Palbox storage is unchanged.
- Chest bonuses still arrive gradually across all base levels. The default cap is now 18; choose 0, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36 or 42. Existing caps round to the nearest step (20 becomes 18; 40 becomes 42). Guild Chests share one bonus.
- Chests show their native capacity plus their extra slots above the grid. Lowering either storage cap keeps occupied extra slots/boxes until they are emptied.
- Range growth now spreads your chosen maximum across all base levels, reaching +100% (double radius) at level 35 by default. The cap remains adjustable from 0 to +300%. Each level uses a whole-percent bonus; the growth-per-level setting is removed.
- Native reward rows, World Settings and tooltips follow the game's 17 languages. Fixed reward-column alignment at 720p.
- Removed the experimental dotted and blue next-boundary previews. The current/next numeric rewards and blue upgrade celebration remain.
- Automatic local save archives with a hard 1 GiB shared archive-directory budget. Clean exits trigger a two-minute quiet period; quick relaunches, unchanged saves and a 30-minute cooldown avoid redundant archives. Baselines and the latest good backups are protected, with recent/daily retention and verified restore copies.
- Windows includes its own private backup companion/runtime. Proton/Linux requires the one-time launcher setup in BACKUPS.txt. No save archiving runs during a managed game session. Windows creates its FIRST baseline AFTER the first clean exit; this does not replace a backup before installing. Servers need a clean stop for a new GG checkpoint.

Before updating
Save and close the game/server, keep an independent world backup, then update the host/server and all clients with the complete archive. Preserve config.lua and world-settings.ini; add new defaults as needed. Keep tools/, backup/ and backup-version.txt with the mod.

Existing bases follow the new range curve immediately and most become smaller. At level 11 with the default cap and a 35 m base, 49 m becomes 45.2 m (52.5 m for the older 5% curve). Check buildings near the boundary. A +136% maximum recreates 4% per upgrade through level 35, but also changes the maximum radius and new-base spacing. Old growth=0 remains off.

Validation
Tested on an isolated dedicated server and an older solo world through Proton/Wine: Palbox expansion, occupied-box retention, saving/reloading Pals in extra boxes, verified backup restoration, the five-row upgrade panel and native-plus-bonus chest labels. Backup lifecycle, quick-relaunch cancellation and restoration were checked on Linux and with Win32 fixtures under Wine. Physical Windows/Steam validation of this beta is still pending. No FPS benchmark is claimed. Native Guild Chest UI and Workshop installation remain unverified.
