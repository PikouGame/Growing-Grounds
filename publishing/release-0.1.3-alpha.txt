0.1.3-alpha — a clearer view, a lighter mod

- Base Range Bonus shows the gain above the original radius, with metres beside each value: 45% 50.8m -> 50% 52.5m.
- Numbers stay aligned with Palworld's reward rows. Smaller mint metre labels and compact percent signs leave room for the arrows.
- The rising contour gradually softens the terrain's sharp relief, with a quicker lift after the bounce. The outward wave keeps its 2.4-second travel time.
- Removed all development keyboard shortcuts, hot reload and profiling code, including when an old config contains debug=true.
- Consolidated text styling, boundary scaling and packaging; removed unused helpers and redundant per-frame material writes.

Update host/server and clients together. Keep your world backup, config.lua and world-settings.ini. No balance, growth, cap or spacing defaults changed in this release.

This remains an alpha. The celebration can still start late after closing the Palbox; actual base growth is unaffected. Lua regression checks pass, but this cleanup has not had a fresh full in-game session. An earlier intermittent client crash is still under investigation; this release is not claimed to fix it.

We'd love to hear how it feels in your world! Leave a comment, share a clip or report a bug so we can investigate and improve it:
